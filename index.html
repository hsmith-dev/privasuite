Privacy, built in

On-device AI

Speech, transcription, summaries, and text recognition run on your device's own processor. Your documents, recordings, and notes are not uploaded to do the work.

Downloads you start

AI models are downloaded only when you ask, from Hugging Face, and checked against fixed checksums. The download never includes your content.

Nothing collected

No accounts, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. We do not receive your data, so there is nothing for us to sell, share, or lose. Read the policy.
