PrivaAssist for Mac

Reads documents and your screen aloud, transcribes and summarizes meetings, documents workflows, and organizes files, with AI that runs on your Mac.

Download PrivaAssist 1.0

Requires macOS 26.6 or later on a Mac with Apple Silicon. Signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Install

  1. Open PrivaAssist.dmg from your Downloads folder.
  2. Drag PrivaAssist onto the Applications folder.
  3. Open PrivaAssist from Applications. The welcome tour shows what each mode does.

First run

  • AI models download only when you ask, from Settings or the first time a feature needs one: the voice (about 340 MB), transcription (about 220 MB), and the language model for summaries (about 3 GB). After that, everything works offline.
  • Permissions are requested only when you press a feature's Request button: Microphone for meetings and dictation, Screen Recording for system audio and on-screen text, Accessibility for Read Screen and Workflow. Skip any you don't need.
  • Hosted models are off. You can turn them on per feature with your own API key; see the privacy policy for exactly what that sends.

Why isn't it on the Mac App Store?

PrivaAssist's Terminal runs a real shell, and Read Screen and Workflow read other apps' text through macOS Accessibility. App Store apps aren't allowed to do either, so PrivaAssist is distributed directly, signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

Updating

PrivaAssist doesn't check for updates in the background, because it makes no network requests you didn't start. Download the latest version from this page and replace the copy in Applications; your documents, settings, and downloaded models are kept.

Uninstalling

Quit PrivaAssist and move it to the Trash. To remove its data and models too, delete ~/Library/Application Support/PrivaAssist.
