Support

Help with PrivaAssist, PrivaPage, PrivaMeet, and PrivaStudy.

Contact us

Email support@harrisonsmith.ai with the app name, your device and system version (for example, iPhone 16, iOS 26), and what happened.

The apps send us nothing, so we can't see your data or logs. Please don't email private documents or recordings; a description or a screenshot of the problem is enough.

All apps

Do I need an account?

No. None of the apps has an account, sign-in, or subscription service of ours.

Why does the app download a model?

The on-device AI models are too large to ship inside the app. Each app downloads only the models you ask for, from Hugging Face, and checks every file against a built-in checksum. You'll see the size before it starts. After that, the feature works offline.

A download failed or says I'm offline

Check your connection and tap Download again; the app keeps what you need to retry. Make sure there is enough free storage for the size shown. If it keeps failing, email us the exact message.

Can I use the apps offline?

Yes. Once the models you use are downloaded, everything runs without a connection. PrivaPage also works with no download at all using the built-in system voices.

How do I back up my data?

The apps don't sync to iCloud or any server. Use each app's Export or Share options to save copies of what you want to keep.

Is the AI output accurate?

It's often good, but models make mistakes. Check transcripts, summaries, and generated cards against the original before relying on them.

PrivaAssist

A feature says it needs a permission

Use the Request button PrivaAssist shows next to the permission, or open System Settings, Privacy & Security, and turn PrivaAssist on under Microphone, Camera, Screen Recording, or Accessibility. Some permissions take effect only after you quit and reopen the app.

How do hosted models work?

They're off unless you turn them on in Settings, per capability, with your own API key. The app shows exactly what will be sent before you confirm, labels hosted output with a Hosted badge, and Offline mode turns all of it off. See the privacy policy.

Can I undo Organize?

Yes. In Organize, use More, then Undo Last Organize, or open History to see what was moved.

PrivaPage

How do I add a PDF?

Tap + (or press Command-O on a Mac), drag a PDF in, or use Open in PrivaPage from the Files app or another app.

A PDF has no text to read

Scanned, image-only pages are recognized on your device, which can take a moment for long documents. If a page still has no text, the scan may be too faint or low-resolution to read.

How big a PDF can I add?

Up to 2 GB and 10,000 pages, so course books are fine. The first time you open a long book, PrivaPage reads its text in the background and shows its progress; you can start listening to the pages it has already read. After that it opens instantly. PrivaPage checks you have enough free storage first and tells you how much it needs.

How do I get the better voice?

Open Settings and download the neural voice (about 340 MB). The system voice keeps working if you skip it.

PrivaMeet

Recording stopped when I locked my iPhone

PrivaMeet keeps recording in the background. If it stopped, check that Microphone access is on in Settings, PrivaMeet, and that another app didn't take over the microphone during a call.

My meeting isn't transcribed yet

Transcription runs on your device after you stop recording and can take a few minutes for long meetings. If the models weren't downloaded yet, tap Download in the banner; waiting meetings are transcribed automatically once they arrive.

App Lock won't turn on

App Lock needs a device passcode, so it never leaves your recordings unprotected when a check can't run. Set a passcode (and Face ID or Touch ID, if you like) in your device's Settings, then try again.

PrivaStudy

How do I make a deck?

Import a PDF or text file, paste an article link, or record a lecture. PrivaStudy drafts cards on your device for you to review and edit.

Can I import a whole textbook?

Yes, up to 2 GB and 10,000 pages. For a PDF over 150 pages, PrivaStudy asks which chapters or pages to make flashcards and notes from. You can still choose the whole book, and you can import it again later for other chapters. Chat and Narrate cover the whole book.

Can I use my decks in Anki?

Yes. PrivaStudy exports decks as Anki .apkg files.

How do I connect Canvas?

Turn on Canvas in PrivaStudy's Settings, then choose Connect and enter your school's Canvas address. Create an access token in Canvas under Account, Settings, New Access Token, and paste it in. If your school doesn't allow student tokens, use the Calendar Feed link from Canvas's Calendar page instead; it brings in assignments and due dates. PrivaStudy only reads from Canvas and never submits anything.

Study reminders aren't showing

Turn on the reminder in PrivaStudy's Settings and allow notifications when asked, or enable them in your device's Settings, Notifications, PrivaStudy.
